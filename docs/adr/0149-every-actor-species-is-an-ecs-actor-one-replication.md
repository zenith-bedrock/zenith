# ADR §149 — Every actor species is an ECS actor; one replication path; the legacy combat backend is gone

> Part of the [decision log](README.md). Code and docs cite this record as `ADR §149`.

**Problem:** Bat and Villager were the last two species on the pre-ECS `IDamageableActor` + `*Store` pattern, and `ProjectileSystem` kept its own copy of the viewer/pose bookkeeping. The ECS docs held them back for two "schema blockers": Bat needing a pitch on `Position`, Villager needing an inventory-shaped component. Both were wrong. Bat's wander heading is state, its yaw is the only rotation it replicates (Fish, already migrated, is the same shape); Villager's "wares" were one item in a list, i.e. two `StackId` values. Keeping them out cost a second combat backend (`GroundMobCombat`), per-pair viewer scans that grew with players x actors, and arrows that could not hit a bat or a villager.

**Choice:** Bat and Villager become `BatState`/`VillagerState` on the shared `EntityRuntime` components, with `MobCore` (spawn, damage, loot, XP) and `ActorReplication` (viewers, pose dedup, batched moves, despawn) like every other species; both register with `DamageDispatch`. `ActorReplication` is generalised over an `ActorAnnouncer` (`ForMob` sends the add-mob packet plus health), so `ProjectileSystem` uses it too, with an add-projectile announcer and no health; `Retire` is the shared "tell the viewers it is gone" step, and `ReconcileAll` serves actors created outside their own species' tick. `Bat`/`Villager`/`BatStore`/`VillagerStore`, `IDamageableActor` and `GroundMobCombat` are deleted; `DamageableActorCombatCore`/`DamageableActorCombat` remain as the one combat path. Bat and Fish now replicate through the shared batched `ReplicateMoves` instead of one packet per move. Bat and Villager data live in `data/mobs.json` like the other species.

**Verified:** 1341/1341 tests (the Bat/Villager tests are ported and extended: damage through the dispatch, the golem-aggro hit memory, a second trade inside the cooldown, replication to a late joiner by counter); live, a client sees `/summon bat` and two `/summon villager` appear and move, and skeleton arrows are announced, moved and removed through the shared path.

**Not verified:** behaviour with a retail client; `smoke:projectile`, `smoke:skeleton` and `smoke:mixed-combat` time out waiting for the first `add_entity`. For `smoke:projectile` this was confirmed to happen **on the commit before this change too** (a worktree of `03271bc`); the other two show the same timeout and were not re-run against the old commit. They could not serve as a regression check and the cause is not investigated here.

**Behaviour changes:** arrows can now hit bats and villagers (as they already could for the other species); bat movement is batched at the end of the tick instead of sent per step.
